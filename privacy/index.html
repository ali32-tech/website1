Last updated: November 11, 2026

This policy explains what information Toolbench ("we", "us") collects when you visit toolbench.co, and how we use it. We keep data collection to a minimum.

Information you give us

If you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address. We use it only to send the newsletter and closely related updates. Every email includes a link to unsubscribe, and we do not sell or rent your address to anyone.

Information collected automatically

Like any website, our hosting provider may record standard server logs, such as your IP address, browser type, and the pages you request. These logs are used to keep the site running and secure.

Third-party services

Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts and code libraries from public content delivery networks. When your browser downloads these files, those providers receive your IP address. Their own privacy policies apply.

Affiliate links and cookies

Some links on Toolbench are affiliate links (see our Affiliate Disclosure). When you click one, the company you visit may set cookies to track the referral. Those cookies are set and controlled by that company, not by us.

Your choices

  • You can unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the link in any email.
  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete the personal data we hold about you.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
